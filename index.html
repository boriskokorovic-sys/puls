In short

  • Puls has no user account and no server. Your bills, amounts, salary and places are stored only on your phone.
  • Puls is free thanks to ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which processes device data as described below. Puls never gives advertisers your bills, amounts, salary or scans.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you choose to: an export, a backup or a transfer to another phone.

Who is responsible

Puls is developed and published by Boris Kokorović. Privacy contact: pulsapp.support@gmail.com.

Data Puls keeps on your phone

  • Places, bills, payments, groups and notes you enter or import.
  • Salary and threshold, if you set them.
  • App settings (language, currency, reminders, protection).
  • Your Puls PIN, if you set one, is stored only as a hash in the phone’s secure storage, never as the number itself.

This data is protected by your phone’s encryption. Puls does not send it to us or anyone else. We have no access to your data.

When data leaves your phone

  • Export (.puls archive, Excel, CSV, PDF): the file goes wherever you send or save it. You can protect the archive with a password.
  • Backup: an encrypted archive you save to a place you choose (e.g. Google Drive, iCloud Drive).
  • Transfer to another phone: directly phone to phone over your local Wi‑Fi, encrypted, with no server and no internet.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Puls uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may process the device’s advertising ID, the IP address and an approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and interactions with ads. Users in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before the first ad (Google’s consent form). You can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy. Without consent you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your phone’s settings. More: How Google uses information.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you open it in Puls (QR transfer, scanning bills). Images are processed on the phone and not sent anywhere.
  • Notifications and calendar: due‑date reminders, on your phone only.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: the phone does the check; Puls only receives “succeeded / failed”.
  • Local network: only for transfers between your phones.

Deletion and your rights

Delete all data in Settings › Delete all data or by deleting the app. Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing else for us to delete or hand over. For data processed by Google, use Google’s settings. For any question or GDPR request, write to the contact above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
